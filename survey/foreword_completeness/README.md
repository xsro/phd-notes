## 核心文献

### 光滑系统
- **Angeli & Sontag (1999)** — *Forward completeness, unboundedness observability, and their Lyapunov characterizations* — Systems & Control Letters. **奠基性论文。**
- **Krstic (2009)** — *Delay Compensation for Nonlinear, Adaptive, and PDE Systems* — Chapter 11: Forward-Complete Systems.

### 非光滑系统（新增）
- **Shevitz & Paden (1994)** — *Lyapunov stability theory of nonsmooth systems* — 非光滑 Lyapunov 稳定性理论的基础
- **Bacciotti & Ceragioli (1999)** — *Stability and stabilization of discontinuous systems and nonsmooth Lyapunov functions*
- **Cortés (2008)** — *Discontinuous dynamical systems: A tutorial* — 非光滑系统 Filippov 解的综合教程
- **Marley, Skjetne, Teel (2023)** — *Sufficient Conditions for UGAS and ISS Using HOCBFs* — 面向微分包含的 forward completeness
