\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec conclusion}

In this paper, a self-organizing fencing control strategy has been proposed for heterogeneous Euler-Lagrange systems with uncertain parameters.
The controller enables the agents to fence a moving target within their convex hull without requiring any predefined formation.
Key features of the proposed method include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Collision avoidance is guaranteed through an artificial potential function that tends to infinity as agents approach the safety distance.
    \item The self-organizing formation naturally adapts to dynamic participation, allowing agents to join or leave the task while the remaining agents continue fencing.
    \item The control framework accommodates parametric uncertainties via adaptive parameter estimation.
\end{itemize}
Lyapunov stability analysis demonstrates that the velocity of the multi-agent system converges to the target's velocity, and the target asymptotically enters the convex hull formed by the agents.
Numerical simulations confirm the effectiveness of the proposed approach.
