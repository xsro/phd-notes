\section{The Decisive Structural Fact: Only the Average Is Constrained}
\label{sec:avg}

Define the position error $\txi_i=x_i-x_0$, the velocity estimation error $\tvell_i=v_i-v_0$, and
their averages
\begin{equation}
  \bx=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i\in\NN}\txi_i,\qquad
  \bv=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i\in\NN}\tvell_i,
  \qquad
  \bu=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i\in\NN}u_i,\qquad
  \tv=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i\in\NN}v_i .
  \label{eq:averages}
\end{equation}
Since the repulsive forces are internal (by symmetry $j\in\calN_i\Leftrightarrow i\in\calN_j$ and
$x_{ij}=-x_{ji}$), they cancel in the sum, $\sum_{i}\phi_i=0$. Substituting \eqref{eq:ctrl7} into
\eqref{eq:veh} and averaging gives
\begin{equation}
  \dot{\bx}=-k_1\bx+\bv,
  \label{eq:avgx}
\end{equation}
and differentiating the observer yields
\begin{equation}
  \dot{\bv}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i\in\NN}\dot{\tvell}_i
        =\frac{k_2}{N}\sum_{i\in\NN}(x_0-x_i)=-k_2\,\bx .
  \label{eq:avgv}
\end{equation}
Combining \eqref{eq:avgx}--\eqref{eq:avgv}, the average position error obeys a single damped
harmonic oscillator
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;\ddot{\bx}+k_1\dot{\bx}+k_2\,\bx=0,\;}
  \label{eq:osc}
\end{equation}
which is exponentially stable because $k_1,k_2>0$. Hence
\begin{equation}
  \bx(t)\to 0,\qquad \dot{\bx}(t)\to 0,\qquad \bv(t)\to 0
  \qquad\text{(exponentially)}.
  \label{eq:conv}
\end{equation}
Because the average position $\bar{x}=\frac1N\sum_i x_i=\bx+x_0$ lies in the convex hull
$\mathrm{co}(x)$, the convergence $\bx\to 0$ proves target fencing (P1). Moreover, since
$\bu=\dot{\bar{x}}+v_0$ and $\tv=\bv+v_0$, we also obtain the \emph{average} velocity tracking
\begin{equation}
  \bu(t)\to v_0,\qquad \tv(t)\to v_0 .
  \label{eq:avgvel}
\end{equation}

The crucial observation is that \eqref{eq:osc} and \eqref{eq:avgvel} constrain \emph{only the
two-dimensional average} quantities $\bx$ and $\bu$. Nothing in the closed loop forces an
\emph{individual} vehicle's velocity $u_i$ or estimate $v_i$ to converge to $v_0$. In particular,
\eqref{eq:avgvel} is compatible with infinitely many velocity patterns: any family $\{u_i\}$ whose
mean equals $v_0$ is admissible, regardless of how the individual $u_i$ behave.
